Rimefall

PLACEHOLDER. This page is written for real in stage 6 and is deliberately thin until then.

It is the only channel for ruleset-specific rulebook prose: genrules takes this file as input and generates the rest of the rulebook from the data tables, and the generator itself is engine code this ruleset does not touch. Anything a player must be told that is not derivable from the tables has to be written here or it goes undocumented.

What stage 6 must cover: the five latitude bands and the starting density curve; how a starting location is claimed through a gateway and what happens when every slot is taken; the two invasion fronts, their sources and what wakes them; the election that ends the game; and above all that starting alliances are a default and not a pact — they carry the full weight of ALLY, they can be dropped one-sidedly and silently, and a faction that assumes otherwise will learn differently at its own expense.
